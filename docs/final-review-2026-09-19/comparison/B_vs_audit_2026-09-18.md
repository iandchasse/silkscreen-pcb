## 1. Summary

The two audits agree far more than they disagree. Of the 113 prior items, **64 map onto one or more new-audit
findings with the same substance** and 2 more are absent only because they were fixed and stayed fixed;
20 are covered in part (usually folded into a larger aggregate finding), 10 rows over 9 distinct topics are
genuine conflicts, and **17 are simply absent from the new audit** (16 of which are still true today and become
recovered findings; the 17th is closed). Six prior items were claimed fixed;
**all six fixes are real and still in the design** (C9 = 4.7 µF/50 V `C513770`, L1 = 47 µH `TYS5040470M-10`/
`C206267`, R14 = 2.2 Ω `C112307`, Q1 manufacturer = EVVOSEMI, HARDWARE.md's CE row and RTC LCSC code) — though
two of them have new problems the prior work could not have known about (R14's LCSC code has collapsed to
12 pieces in stock; C9 is still only marginally above TI's 1 µF effective minimum). One claimed fix, the
battery-path widening recorded in `CHECKPOINT_2.md` §4, was **applied but left incomplete** — the
`Q3 → R27 → Q8` link is still 0.25 mm, which the new audit catches as BAT-07/PWR-V01.

The prior corpus's biggest blind spot is the new audit's only BLOCKER. **Nothing anywhere in corpus B mentions
solder-paste apertures on the FH34SRJ FPC connectors.** The word "paste" appears in corpus B only for the
TP4056/ESP32 thermal-pad vias and, ironically, in `j2-mount-pads-oversize-vs-hirose`, which reasons that "JLC
paste will print the full 1.5 × 1.5 mm pad" — true of J2's *shield* tabs, which do carry paste, while the 36
*signal* pads of J2/J3/J4 carry none. The prior audit looked at that exact footprint and never checked the pad
layer sets. **LED-01 is genuinely new.**

Going the other way, the new audit dropped a coherent cluster of prior work: the **Fix-4 / DW01A recovery
behaviour** (a 0 V or protection-latched pack cannot be revived; after any DW01A trip the board cannot restart
from the cell alone because Q8's gate is tied to system GND), plus about ten measured footprint/layout details
the prior corpus had quantified. Those become **REC-B-01 … REC-B-12** below. Only three are above LOW.

Cost: the prior `cost_model.py` output ($33.23 / $22.70 / $16.25 / $12.61 per board at 5/10/30/100, 26 Extended
part types) is **explicitly corroborated by the new §12**, which calls BOM.md's 26-Extended figure "the one to
use" (BOM-16) and reconstructs ≈ $47/board all-in at 5 boards on top of it. No conflict.

## 3. Conflicts and adjudications

**C1 — Sustained VBUS overvoltage margin (F1/CR1).**
*Prior:* owner dismissed it — the board never negotiates PD, only presents Rd, so a compliant source cannot
impose 9–20 V. *New:* USB-V01/USB-04 — only 0.5 V separates the highest legal USB-C rail (5.5 V) from three
downstream absolute maxima (6.0 V), so no TVS exists that can protect this rail; USB-05 adds that F1's 1.00 A
hold has little margin over the ~0.86 A worst case.
**Both partly right.** The prior reasoning about compliant sources is correct and the new audit does not refute
it; the new audit is right that the rail has essentially no protection margin for a *non-compliant* supply, a
faulty charger or the hot-plug ring (which the prior corpus itself kept open as
`vbus-hotplug-overshoot-not-clamped`). The final report should say: the sustained-PD-fault scenario is not
reachable from a compliant source, but the rail is nonetheless unprotected against any abnormal excursion, and
no clamp part fits the 5.5–6.0 V window. MEDIUM, first-article scope check.

**C2 — The 1.30 mm bottom flex slot.**
*Prior:* `bottom-fpc-slot-1p3mm-ok` — "47 × 1.3 mm bottom slot is within JLC capability". *New:* MEC-01 (HIGH)
— "flex slot is 1.30 mm wide, narrower than the 1.60 mm board, and is the only path for all three display
flexes".
**Both partly right — they answer different questions.** The prior statement is a *fabrication* judgement and is
correct (1.3 mm clears JLC's 1.0 mm routed-slot minimum; the new audit's own FAB-23 agrees this fifth polygon
"clears every fab"). The new statement is a *mechanical* judgement about routing three FPCs through a slot
narrower than the board is thick. MEC-01 stands; the prior "OK" must not be quoted as clearing it. The final
report should carry both sentences explicitly so the two are not confused.

**C3 — Via-in-pad under solder paste (U4 vs U11).**
*Prior:* two separate findings — `tp4056-epad-vias-under-paste` (4 of 6 U11 vias fall *inside* B.Paste windows,
2 clear by only ~0.07 mm) and `esp32-epad-vias-windowpaned-ok` (0 of 12 U4 vias fall inside any of the nine
0.9 × 0.9 mm paste windows — U4 is correctly windowpaned). *New:* FAB-V01 (LOW) — "18 unfilled via-in-pad holes
inside the exposed thermal pads of U4 and U11".
**Prior is right and more precise.** Lumping the two parts overstates U4's risk and understates U11's, and loses
the actionable fix (move U11's vias into the gaps, the way U4 already does). Both audits agree the consequence
at U11 is degraded thermal joint quality, not a broken net. → **REC-B-02** restores the distinction.

**C4 — Width of the four tongue-neck slots (internal to the new audit).**
*Prior:* "~0.5–0.6 mm", below JLC's 1.0 mm minimum. *New:* FAB-23 headline says **0.716 mm**, LAY-01 says
**0.50 mm**, MEC-03 says **0.516 mm**, and FAB-23's own verifier note opens "THE NUMBERS ARE WRONG" and
re-measures 0.5159 mm.
**Prior is right.** 0.716 mm is the polygon bounding box including the 0.20 mm Edge.Cuts line width; the
centre-line slot is 0.516 mm. The final report must publish **0.52 mm** and delete the 0.716 mm figure and
FAB-23's derived lengths (1.525/2.025/2.025/2.725 mm → actually 1.325/1.825/2.525/1.825 mm). The conclusion —
below JLC's and PCBWay's minimums, within NextPCB's — is unaffected.

**C5 — The USB/charge-status ADC divider.**
*Prior:* `usb-stat-divider-verified-correct` — "fully verified correct against all 4 documented voltage states"
(a positive confirmation). *New:* USB-01 (the > 3.10 V idle window is above ATTEN3's 2900 mV calibrated range
and can never be reported), USB-02 (three physical states collapse into the same 0.50–0.70 V window), USB-V02,
USB-14, USB-18, USB-19.
**New is right.** The prior check verified the divider *arithmetic* against the states the documentation lists;
it never asked whether the ESP32-S3 can read the resulting voltage, nor whether distinct states map to distinct
voltages. That is a scope error, not a computational one, but the practical effect is that corpus B certified a
status channel that is partly unreadable and partly ambiguous. The final report should carry USB-01/USB-02 and
explicitly retire the prior "verified correct" line.

**C6 — ERC warnings.**
*Prior:* `erc-warnings-all-noise-or-cosmetic` — "All 39 ERC warnings are library/footprint-filter cosmetic noise
… zero real". *New:* `evidence/sch/erc.json` reports 85 violations at `--severity-all`, and FAB-13 (one wire
carries two names, `PIN_3` and `TP_RST`, on J4 pin 3) plus LED-09 (cached symbols for U10 and J3 differ from
their libraries) are genuine.
**Both partly right.** The counts differ only because of `--severity-all` vs the default severity set, and the
new audit agrees none of these break the netlist (both rated LOW). But "zero real" was too strong: the J4.3
double-name and the two symbol mismatches are real bookkeeping defects. Final wording: no ERC violation affects
connectivity; three are worth fixing as hygiene.

**C7 — "Floating" GND copper.**
*Prior:* `floating-copper-overstated-in-precomputed-metric` — "No truly disconnected GND copper islands exist on
the board". *New:* LAY-05 (B.Cu ground is 50 fill areas, 14 with no via to F.Cu; the verifier re-ran it and got
51 areas, 19 with no via, 282 mm²), LAY-V01 (the tongue's 120.2 mm² island has zero stitching vias), FAB-06
("connected to isolated island").
**Both right about different properties.** Nothing is electrically disconnected — the new audit's own
`drc.json` shows `unconnected_items = 0`, which is exactly the prior claim. What the new audit adds is that many
B.Cu fill areas have no *interlayer* via, which is a return-path/RF property, not a connectivity one. Final
report: keep LAY-05/LAY-V01 but do not describe these areas as "floating copper"; say "no via to the opposite
plane".

**C8 — BOOT button SW6 is DNP.**
*Prior:* informational — DNP by design, but the THT footprint pads remain present and reachable for manual
recovery. *New:* MCU-01, MEDIUM (was HIGH) — no fitted way into download mode when USB-Serial/JTAG is
unavailable, which for a device that lives in deep sleep is a realistic bricking path.
**Both partly right.** The new audit's risk analysis is sound (ESP-IDF documents the deep-sleep USB-Serial/JTAG
disconnect and the pin-reconfiguration failure mode), and the prior audit never weighed that. The prior audit's
mitigation is also real and should be kept in the recommendation: the pads exist, so recovery is a pair of
tweezers, not a rework. MEDIUM is the right grade.

**C9 — Q1's Manufacturer field.**
*Prior:* claimed **FIXED** — manufacturer corrected from Fortune Semiconductor (whose FS8205A is TSSOP-8 only)
to EVVOSEMI, a genuine SOT-23-6 FS8205A that DigiKey stocks. *New:* BAT-V03 — manufacturer/datasheet fields for
U5 and Q1 do not match the parts their LCSC codes deliver.
**Both partly right, no defect.** `fabrication/part_fields.csv` reads
`Q1,FS8205A,FS8205A,EVVOSEMI,C2830320,,TECH PUBLIC FS8205A,…` — the Manufacturer column is DigiKey-primary by
the repo's own convention (recorded in this repo's memory as a standing rule), and the LCSC-delivered maker is
in the substitute column. The original package error is genuinely fixed. BAT-V03 is a documentation-convention
nit: the fields would read better if the column headings said which side they describe.

## 7. Blind spots

**Corpus B's blind spots (what the new audit found because B did not look).**
1. **Pad layer sets.** B never enumerated which SMD pads carry B.Paste. That single omission is the BLOCKER.
2. **The reader, not the board.** B had no end-user or README review at all. The new audit's 24 DOCS findings,
   9 of them HIGH, are entirely outside B's scope — and they are the findings most likely to stop a stranger
   from building this board.
3. **Three-dimensional mechanics.** B measured the board in plan view. It never asked whether the panel sits
   flat on 58 THT leads (MEC-23), whether an inserted microSD card passes through the battery bay (SD-V01), or
   whether three flexes fit a 1.3 mm slot in a 1.6 mm board (MEC-01) — and in that last case it actively
   cleared the geometry on fab grounds.
4. **Sensor range, not just sensor arithmetic.** B verified the USB-status divider's voltages and stopped,
   missing that the ESP32-S3 cannot read the top state and that several states are degenerate (C5).
5. **Part choice as a cost/function decision.** B priced the BOM accurately but never asked whether the DS3231MZ
   (25 % of the DigiKey cost) or five ESD arrays are the right parts to have chosen.
6. **JLC-centric.** B reasoned almost entirely from the JLC build and the fab outputs; the new audit's hand-build
   and other-fab angles (FAB-15, FAB-19, BOM-08, BOM-15) have little counterpart in B.

**The new audit's blind spots (what corpus B had that it lost).**
1. **Battery-subsystem recovery behaviour.** The whole Fix-4 / DW01A "how does this board come back to life"
   thread — dead pack, post-trip lockout, Q8's gate on system GND — is missing (REC-B-01, REC-B-03). It is the
   most consequential gap, because it is user-visible and undocumented.
2. **Precision lost to aggregation.** FAB-V01 merges U4 and U11 and loses the fix (C3); FAB-06/LAY-12/FAB-09
   fold B's individually measured silk and thermal items into counts, which is more readable but drops the
   per-item coordinates B had.
3. **Measured footprint details.** U10's pad width, J2's shield land vs Hirose, the near-edge passive cluster,
   the PREVGH stub, L2's Isat margin, SW2 vs H4 — all measured by B, none restated (REC-B-06 … REC-B-11).
4. **One wrong number that its own verifier caught.** FAB-23's 0.716 mm slot width contradicts LAY-01, MEC-03,
   the verifier note and B. Do not publish it (C4).
5. **Simulation and part-by-part substitution analysis.** B ran an LTspice corner simulation of the
   reverse-battery gate and built a datasheet-vs-datasheet table for every LCSC substitute at its actual
   operating point. The new audit does neither; BOM-09 reaches a similar conclusion ("untraceable clone-market
   silicon") by a much coarser route.
6. **Fab-output freshness is proven only in the evidence pack.** The new audit verified that the committed
   Gerbers and CPL match the board (`gerber_fresh/COMPARISON.txt`) but raised no finding, so a reader of the
   report alone cannot tell that B's old HIGH item was checked and closed. The final report should say so
   explicitly.
